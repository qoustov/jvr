Read the transcript 1 minute

All children dream of becoming adults as soon as possible. Visiting KidZania, you will see how sincerely and boldly they get immersed in our lives.

KidZania is an adult world created specifically for kids. It looks and operates like a real city. Here, children try their hand at more than 100 exciting professions, acquire useful skills and unforgettable experiences.

The idea to prepare children for adult life in a playful way, which is at the core of the KidZania concept, correlates with the global objectives of Royal Canin. Among those are the popularization of the veterinarian profession, actualization of the need for preventive visits to the veterinary clinic and education of a responsible attitude to pets.

The veterinary clinic opening will teach children in an interactive way to take care of their pets’ health properly and consciously. Different scenarios of activity in the veterinary clinic are developed for children aged four to fourteen years.

Meet Jennifer

The stage has always felt like home.

Hi, I’m Jennifer Victoria—a Brooklyn-born performer with a lifelong love for the stage. I started playing piano at six, joined choirs, trained in multiple dance styles for seven years, and performed anywhere with a spotlight—or at least decent acoustics.

With more than a decade of voice acting experience, I’ve brought characters to life in everything from cartoons to video games. I’ve also performed in musical theatre, orchestras and vocal showcases, blending my passions for music, movement and storytelling.

Whether I’m singing, acting, dancing or voicing a new character, I’m all about creating performances that entertain, inspire and leave a little sparkle behind.
